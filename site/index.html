      FAQ

      What is openpaper?

      A trading account that runs on your own computer: your real exchange, broker, wallet and prediction-market accounts on one page, a paper account with live prices, and AI agents that trade, move money and pay inside limits you sign; you watch, steer and approve.

      Is this real money?

      When you run it, yes: real orders at the accounts you connect, each under a cap you set, with a read-only switch. The code has only ever been exercised against stand-ins.

      Where does it run?

      On your own machine, for one operator. Keys stay in your files, the owner is a key in your browser, and nothing is hosted.
